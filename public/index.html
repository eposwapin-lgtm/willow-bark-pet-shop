Our Store

A neighborhood pet shop on Gallatin Avenue.

Willow & Bark opened in 2016 when Dana Whitfield and Marcus Lee adopted a scruffy rescue named Willow and couldn't find a single shop in East Nashville that could answer a straight question about her food. So they built one on Gallatin Avenue.

Ten years later the rule still holds: nothing on the shelf that we wouldn't feed Willow, and nobody behind the counter who can't explain why it's there. We also host free adoption days with Nashville Humane every first Saturday.
